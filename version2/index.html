Final-Year Project

Hardware-Efficient Resolver Angle Decoding

Sep 2026 – PresentSupervisor Dr. Jing Li

The project compares ways to recover shaft angle from a resolver’s sine and cosine signals efficiently in digital hardware.

  • Developing MATLAB decoders and comparing floating-point and fixed-point versions for angle accuracy, numerical precision, and implementation complexity.
  • Next, implement and validate the selected method on an FPGA.

Methods under evaluation

  • CORDIC
  • Lookup table (LUT)
  • Rational approximation
  1. ResolverSine and cosine inputs
  2. MATLABDecoder comparison
  3. FPGAImplementation and validation planned
