Final-year project

Hardware‑Efficient Resolver Angle Decoding

Sep 2026 – Present · Supervisor Dr. Jing Li

A resolver produces sine and cosine signals that encode shaft angle. My project compares ways to recover that angle efficiently for a digital hardware implementation.

I’m developing MATLAB decoders and comparing their floating-point and fixed-point implementations for angle accuracy, numerical precision, and implementation complexity. The next step is to implement and validate the selected method on an FPGA.

Methods under evaluation

  • CORDIC
  • Lookup table (LUT)
  • Rational approximation
